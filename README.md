# NASA & Adler Planetarium Exoplanet Research

## Overview
* **Project:** Astronomy Research Collaboration analyzing exoplanets using raw telescope data and citizen science pipelines (NASAs Exoplanet Watch).
* **Result:** **Co-authored a high-level APA research paper** and presented findings in class; successfully extracted light curves from raw data like professional astronomers.
* **Partners & Tools:** Chicago Adler Planetarium (Doane Observatory) telescope data, NASA Exoplanet Watch program, Google Colab (Python), and EXOTIC software.
* **Program:** College of DuPage & Adler Planetarium Exoplanet Research Initiative Fall 2025.

## Operational Responsibilities & Contributions
* **Data Processing & Photometry:** Processed 165 raw FITS images and calibration frames (biases, darks, and flats) from Adler’s PlaneWave CDK24 telescope to measure star brightness over time.
* **Pipeline Execution & Troubleshooting:** Configured and troubleshot NASA's EXOTIC Python-based notebook within Google Colab, mapping target coordinates, comparison stars, and observatory parameters (latitude, longitude, altitude).
* **Light Curve Generation & Analysis:** Successfully generated and visualized transit light curves for the WTS-2 exoplanet system, validating dips in star brightness to detect exoplanet transit data.
* **Research & Academic Collaboration:** Partnered with Adler Planetarium officials (Michelle Nichols, Hunter Miller) and College of DuPage faculty (Professors Joseph Dal Santo, Kristie Nault) to bridge academic astronomy with practical data science workflows.

## Technical Stack & Environment
* **Data Environment:** Google Colab, Python, NASA EXOTIC (EXOplanet Transit Interpretation Code)
* **Hardware & Formats:** Adler's PlaneWave CDK24 (24”) reflecting telescope, Flexible Image Transport System (.fits) astronomical data format
* **Core Disciplines:** Astronomical photometry, data calibration, light curve analysis, scientific research writing, public-private STEM collaboration
